What the game simplifies

Logical mass grows through compressed thresholds. Displayed astronomical units do not imply that every numerical ratio is an exact physical model. Time, size, distance and movement are scaled for play. Gravity is softened and limited by distance; stable systems use approximate orbital hierarchies. Gas uses coarse cells and bounded matter parcels. Lensing is a visual displacement approximation. Companions and asteroid DNA are fictional gameplay content. Strange Matter, gravastars and The Interstallar are speculative or fictional game forms. Optional supernova routes preserve mass, and their compressed evolution thresholds do not model actual stellar endings.

Engineering interface and gravastar references

The publisher’s Diep.io game and changelog informed the compact colored rank bars and direct upgrade selection. ACCRETION uses its own layout, branch names, requirements, artwork and code. No Diep.io assets are bundled.

Kurzgesagt: Black Hole’s Evil Twin, Gravastars Explained is the requested visual and conceptual reference. The underlying Mazur and Mottola gravastar proposal describes a vacuum-energy interior separated from the exterior by a thin material boundary, without a singularity or event horizon. The game now draws a distinct interior and moving surface waves. This is an original interpretation of a hypothetical model, not a confirmed object or a simulation of quantum gravity.

Mining currency, research timing, generators, battery units, combat and overclock bonuses are compressed game rules. Initial stellar systems use orbital velocities; their later stability is decided by the existing simulation. Galactic associations use a few aggregate bodies and nested stars rather than thousands of simulated stars.

Black-hole optics and original rendering

Version 3.9 adds seven original stage-specific animated Canvas designs, drawn from paths in final-render.js. No photograph or imported black-hole asset is used. Distinct inclinations, flow arms and feeding-dependent jets are artistic gameplay choices; class mass alone does not determine the appearance of a real accretion flow. See design notes.

Black holes in this game are an original real-time rendering inspired by published scientific visualisation. No film footage, images or sound are included. The optional renderer solves a bounded Schwarzschild null-orbit model. Its weak-field point-source lens, finite photosphere, warm grade and display asymmetry are gameplay approximations. It is not DNGR or a full Kerr simulation.
