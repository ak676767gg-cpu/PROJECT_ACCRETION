Privacy policy

Updated 7 October 2026. Applies to the supplied offline build, version 4.0.0.

Solo play runs on your device without sending gameplay, companion names or saved progress to a server. Multiplayer connects only when you explicitly choose a server and press Connect. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising tools or payment forms in this build.

Information stored on your device

The game uses your browser's local storage under the key accretion.v1. This is browser storage, not a tracking cookie. It contains:

  • Your selected mode/save slot and each slot's saved universe, progress, upgrades, resources and checkpoints.
  • Companions, optional nicknames, incubating eggs, achievements and personal records.
  • Gameplay statistics such as time played, objects captured and highest mass reached.
  • Audio, display, movement and difficulty settings.
  • Galaxy visits, estimated depletion, plotted course and last-observed rival positions.
  • Companion evolution experience and branch choices, composition and stellar weather state.
  • Earned appearance choices, prestige ranks and retired-journey history, optional expedition promises and orbital blueprint goals.
  • Saved impact marks on bodies and their orientation.
  • Final-horizon position, discovery, closest approach, photos counted, merger progress and local rendering preferences.
  • The latest recorded encounter, with a bounded set of nearby-body positions.
  • Separate sandbox, boss and salvage scenario profiles.
  • Four custom signals, their optional local image/GIF data, text and shape choices.

These details let the game restore a journey and apply your preferences. Nicknames are optional and appear locally. You do not need to provide your real name, email, age, location or any other identifying detail.

Collection, sharing and network access

Solo modes do not transmit storage. Their leaderboards contain simulated rivals. Optional multiplayer sends your chosen pilot name, room code, control inputs, purchases and messages to the server you select. Signals you send can include your selected local image or GIF and are relayed to other pilots in that room. The server and room participants receive those messages; other players’ media is hidden by default. Your browser also exposes connection metadata, including its network address, to the host. The supplied server keeps rooms and chat in memory, without a database or gameplay log. A different host or network proxy may retain its own logs. Consult that host before connecting. Campaign saves, companion collections and local achievements are not uploaded. There are no analytics, tracking pixels, cloud saves or automatic update requests.

All game scripts, images, styles and sounds are supplied locally or generated on your device. The Sources page contains external reference links. They are opened only when you select them; the destination website then handles that visit under its own policies. This notice does not describe your browser, operating system or the service used to obtain the ZIP.

Photo exports

Photo mode creates a PNG locally and offers it as a browser download. The game does not upload the image or retain a copy in save data. Manage exported images in your browser downloads or file manager.

Private developer comparison

The optional rendering lab reads an image that you select from your device using FileReader. The image stays in that page memory. It is not uploaded, written to local storage, or included in the lab PNG export. Closing the page clears it. Diagnostic pages use an isolated in-memory save.

Retention, correction and deletion

Saved data remains until you remove it or your browser clears it. A browser may also remove storage in private mode, when storage limits are reached or when you clear browsing data. This build has no remote backup or account recovery.

You can correct a companion nickname in Life Lab and change preferences in Settings. To remove all saved data, close every ACCRETION tab and clear the corresponding local/site storage through your browser's settings or storage tools. This deletes all three journey slots, the sandbox and scenario profiles, custom signals, replays, companion collections and settings. There is no undo inside the game.

Local file storage behavior differs between browsers. Moving or renaming the game folder, changing browser profiles, or later opening a hosted version may show a different storage area. Keep the same browser and folder when updating if you want to retain existing saves.

Access on shared devices

Anyone with access to the same browser profile may be able to view or alter its game data. Companion names and local saves are not encrypted by this application. Use a separate browser profile if you share a device.

Scope and future online use

No public domain, named website operator, hosting provider or public contact channel has been configured for this build. It is not being offered here as a hosted service. Before public hosting, the operator must publish their real contact details and describe any hosting logs, service providers, retention practices and applicable rights. This offline notice does not certify compliance for a future hosted site.

Custom signals can be edited or removed in the signal editor. Use only media you intend to share when sending multiplayer messages. The access key is kept in the connection form, not saved into the game vault.

The actual storage implementation is available in save.js and life.js. Relevant browser documentation and policy references are listed on the Sources page.
