The game

ACCRETION provides offline single-player modes and an optional private multiplayer arena. It models gravitational growth, orbital systems and accretion using compressed gameplay rules. It is entertainment, not a scientific simulation or professional advice. Computer-controlled rivals, astronomical events and companions are simulated locally.

This build does not contain purchases, subscriptions, paid upgrades, advertising or payment collection. No external account or internet connection is required to play the extracted files.

Optional multiplayer and custom signals

Multiplayer requires a separately operated server and an explicit connection. Shared rooms do not pause when menus are open. Do not send private information or media you lack permission to share. Use the signal visibility settings to hide text/media. The supplied backend does not provide public matchmaking, persistent accounts or moderation staff. Each host is responsible for their server and any additional terms or data practices. No public service is launched by this ZIP.

Game resources and companions

Water and DNA are earned through play. They cannot be purchased with money, transferred to other players or redeemed for cash. Companion rarity describes gameplay outcomes, not monetary value.

The discovery wheel costs 500 water and 100 DNA for a successful discovery. Its sectors are Common 59%, Rare 35%, Epic 5% and Legendary 1%. The Common sector also includes the existing Uncommon companions. Outcomes are selected locally; no rarity is guaranteed on an individual spin. A completed sector spends no resources and a full incubator blocks further spins. The Life Lab shows the same rules during play.

Local saves

Three save slots store separate progress on the device. With the three-evolution toggle enabled, automatic checkpoints occur after three new astronomical classes. Save now, returning to the main menu using Save & return, and changing slots save immediately. Charting The Final Interstallar and completing its merger also make explicit full checkpoints. Closing between checkpoints can discard later progress. The Privacy policy explains storage, retention and deletion.

There is no promised cloud recovery or transfer between devices. Moving the game, clearing storage or changing browser profiles can make a previous save unavailable. Keep a copy of your existing game folder before replacing files.
