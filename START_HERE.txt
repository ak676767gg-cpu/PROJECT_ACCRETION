ACCRETION 4.0.0 · Orbital Command
© 2026 Web Stars Studios

1. Extract the entire ZIP.
2. Open the Accretion folder.
3. Double-click index.html.

Solo play needs no installation, server, Node.js, account or internet.
Keep all files together. Sound starts after a click.

WASD / arrows: thrust. Shift: brake. Space: unlocked boost.
F: open dock, which hides after inactivity. H: hide/restore HUD.
U: original adaptations. K: engineering skill trees.
X: aimed weapons. Z: drone orders. I: personal mining laser.
T: quick signals. Y: send first saved signal. Edit them in the T panel. Escape: pause/back.

Game modes: Journey, Boss Gauntlet, Salvage Expedition, Sandbox,
and an optional private Multiplayer Arena.

Settings: performance and resolution controls are at the bottom.
FPS slider: 30 to 240 FPS, then Unlimited. Hardware limits still apply.
Separate animation switches, CPU budgets, shader and gas detail are available.

Read ORBITAL_COMMAND.html for the update and MULTIPLAYER.html for the
optional backend. Only the multiplayer host needs Python 3.10+.
Existing saved journeys, pets and Hardcore caches remain supported.
